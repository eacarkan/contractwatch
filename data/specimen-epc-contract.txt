SPECIMEN EPC CONTRACT
A synthetic contract written for demonstration. It follows the general order of an international design-build form, in original wording, and is not a copy or summary of any published form.

1 General matters

1.1 Language and communications
Every notice under this Contract shall be in writing and in English. A notice takes effect on the day it is received at the address stated in the Contract Data.

1.2 Change of address
A Party that changes its address for notices shall tell the other Party of the new address within 7 days of the change.

2 The Employer

2.1 Access to the Site
The Employer shall give the Contractor access to the whole of the Site within 30 days after the Commencement Date. If the Employer has not done so by then, the Contractor may treat the delay as an Employer Delay Event.

2.2 Evidence of funding
Within 21 days after a written request from the Contractor, the Employer shall provide reasonable evidence that funds are available to pay the Contract Price.

3 The Employer's Representative

3.1 Appointment
The Employer shall name the Employer's Representative within 10 days after the date of this Contract.

3.2 Replacing the Representative
The Employer shall give the Contractor at least 14 days' notice before replacing the Employer's Representative.

3.3 Replies to submissions
The Employer's Representative shall answer any request for a decision within 14 days of receiving it. A submission not answered within that period is treated as having no objection.

4 The Contractor

4.1 Performance Security
The Contractor shall deliver the Performance Security to the Employer within 28 days after the Commencement Date.

4.2 Keeping the security valid
Where the Performance Security would expire before the Works are complete, the Contractor shall extend it not later than 30 days before its expiry date.

4.3 Key Personnel
The Contractor shall submit the names and curricula of its Key Personnel within 15 days after the Commencement Date.

4.4 Subcontractors
The Contractor shall notify the Employer's Representative of each proposed Subcontractor at least 21 days before that Subcontractor starts work on the Site.

5 Design

5.1 Design programme
The Contractor shall submit a design delivery schedule within 20 days after the Commencement Date.

5.2 Errors in the Employer's Requirements
If the Contractor finds an error in the Employer's Requirements, it shall notify the Employer's Representative within 10 days of finding it. A Contractor that does not give this notice in time shall have no right to additional time or money arising from that error.

5.3 As-built documents
The Contractor shall hand over the as-built drawings and operating manuals no later than 45 days before the date of the Completion Tests.

6 Staff and Labour

6.1 Site safety reports
The Contractor shall send a site safety report to the Employer's Representative every 30 days.

6.2 Accidents
The Contractor shall report any serious accident to the Employer's Representative within 2 working days after it happens.

7 Plant, Materials and Workmanship

7.1 Inspection notice
The Contractor shall give the Employer's Representative at least 5 working days' notice before covering any work that is to be inspected.

7.2 Rejected Plant
The Contractor shall replace or repair rejected Plant within 30 days of being told of the rejection.

8 Commencement, Delays and Suspension

8.1 Commencement of Works
The Contractor shall begin the Works within 14 days after the Commencement Date.

8.2 Programme
The Contractor shall submit a detailed programme within 28 days after the Commencement Date.

8.3 Progress reports
The Contractor shall deliver a progress report to the Employer's Representative every 30 days until the Works are taken over.

8.4 Time for Completion
The Contractor shall complete the Works within the Time for Completion stated in the Contract Data.

8.5 Early warning of delay
The Contractor shall warn the Employer's Representative within 7 days of learning of any event likely to delay the Works.

8.6 Notice of suspension
The Contractor shall give the Employer at least 21 days' notice before suspending work for non-payment.

9 Tests on Completion

9.1 Notice of readiness
The Contractor shall give the Employer's Representative at least 21 days' notice of the date on which it will be ready for the Tests on Completion.

9.2 Repeat tests
If a test fails, the Employer's Representative may require it to be repeated, and the Contractor shall repeat the test within 14 days of that request.

10 Employer's Taking Over

10.1 Taking-Over Certificate
The Employer's Representative shall issue the Taking-Over Certificate or give reasons for refusing it within 21 days of receiving the Contractor's application.

11 Defects Liability

11.1 Defects Notification Period
The Contractor shall remedy any defect notified during the Defects Notification Period within 14 days of the notice.

11.2 Performance Certificate
The Employer's Representative shall issue the Performance Certificate within 28 days after the end of the Defects Notification Period.

12 Variations

12.1 Variation proposals
Within 14 days of receiving a Variation request, the Contractor shall submit a proposal stating its effect on the programme and the Contract Price.

12.2 Objection to an instruction
A Contractor that considers an instruction to be a Variation shall say so in writing within 10 days of receiving it, failing which the instruction shall be treated as not being a Variation.

13 Contract Price and Payment

13.1 Advance payment
The Employer shall pay the advance payment within 30 days after receiving the Performance Security and the advance payment guarantee.

13.2 Payment applications
The Contractor shall submit a payment application at the end of every 30 days.

13.3 Interim certificates
The Employer's Representative shall certify each payment application within 21 days of receiving it.

13.4 Interim payments
The Employer shall pay each certified amount within 45 days of the date of the certificate.

13.5 Final statement
The Contractor shall submit a draft final statement within 56 days after receiving the Performance Certificate.

14 Termination

14.1 Notice to correct
Where the Contractor is in material breach, the Employer may require it to correct the breach within 30 days of a notice to correct.

14.2 Termination by the Contractor
The Contractor may terminate this Contract by giving 28 days' notice if an amount certified remains unpaid 60 days after it fell due.

15 Risk and Insurance

15.1 Evidence of insurance
The Contractor shall deliver evidence of the required insurances within 21 days after the Commencement Date.

15.2 Insurance renewals
The Contractor shall deliver evidence of each renewal at least 7 days before the current policy ends.

16 Force Majeure

16.1 Notice of Force Majeure
A Party prevented from performing by Force Majeure shall notify the other Party within 14 days of becoming aware of the event, and shall have no relief for any period before the notice if it does not.

17 Claims and Disputes

17.1 Contractor's claims
If the Contractor considers itself entitled to additional time or money, it shall notify the Employer's Representative within 28 days of becoming aware of the event. If the Contractor does not give notice within that period, it shall lose its right to additional time and money for that event.

17.2 Particulars of a claim
Within 42 days of becoming aware of the event, the Contractor shall send the Employer's Representative full particulars of the claim.

17.3 Response to a claim
The Employer's Representative shall respond to the particulars within 42 days of receiving them.

17.4 Notice of dissatisfaction
A Party dissatisfied with a decision shall give notice of dissatisfaction within 28 days of receiving it. If neither Party gives this notice in time, the decision becomes final and binding.

17.5 Amicable settlement
The Parties shall attempt to settle a dispute amicably for 30 days after a notice of dissatisfaction before starting arbitration.
